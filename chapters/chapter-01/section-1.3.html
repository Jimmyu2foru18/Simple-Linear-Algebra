Section 1.3: Vector Equations

Assigned problems: 4, 5, 13. Source: linearAlgebraSolutionsComplete.pdf.

What is this section about?

What is a vector equation? A vector equation has the form x₁v₁ + x₂v₂ + … + x₀v₀ = b, where v₁, …, v₀ are vectors and x₁, …, x₀ are scalars. It is equivalent to a system of linear equations.

Core Lecture, Gilbert Strang (MIT)

Concept Overview

A vector equation has the form:

$$x_1\mathbf{v}_1 + x_2\mathbf{v}_2 + \cdots + x_p\mathbf{v}_p = \mathbf{b}$$

This is equivalent to the linear system whose augmented matrix is $[\mathbf{v}_1 \, \mathbf{v}_2 \, \cdots \, \mathbf{v}_p \, | \, \mathbf{b}]$.

Vector $\mathbf{b}$ is a linear combination of $\mathbf{v}_1, \dots, \mathbf{v}_p$ if and only if the augmented matrix has a solution.

Worked Problems from linearAlgebraSolutionsComplete.pdf

Problem 4

Problem: Given vectors $\mathbf{u}$ and $\mathbf{v}$ in $\mathbb{R}^2$, draw the vectors $\mathbf{u} - \mathbf{v}$, $\mathbf{u}$, $\mathbf{v}$, $\mathbf{u} + \mathbf{v}$, $-\mathbf{v} - 2\mathbf{v}$, and $\mathbf{u} - 2\mathbf{v}$ on the coordinate grid.

Solution

Using the geometric grid:

  • $\mathbf{u} - \mathbf{v}$: travel from origin along $\mathbf{u}$, then backward along $\mathbf{v}$
  • $\mathbf{u} + \mathbf{v}$: travel from origin along $\mathbf{u}$, then forward along $\mathbf{v}$
  • $-\mathbf{v} - 2\mathbf{v} = -3\mathbf{v}$: travel 3 units in the negative $\mathbf{v}$-direction
  • $\mathbf{u} - 2\mathbf{v}$: travel from origin along $\mathbf{u}$, then 2 units backward along $\mathbf{v}$

Problem 5

Problem: Compute $A\mathbf{x}$ where $A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 6 \\ 1 & 4 & 7 \\ 5 & 0 & 5 \end{bmatrix}$ and $\mathbf{x} = \begin{bmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \\ x_3 \end{bmatrix}$. Write the result as a linear combination of the columns of $A$.

Solution

$$A\mathbf{x} = x_1\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \\ 5 \end{bmatrix} + x_2\begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 4 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix} + x_3\begin{bmatrix} 6 \\ 7 \\ 5 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} x_1 + 2x_2 + 6x_3 \\ x_1 + 4x_2 + 7x_3 \\ 5x_1 + 0x_2 + 5x_3 \end{bmatrix}$$

Problem 13

Problem: Denote the columns of $A$ by $\mathbf{a}_1, \mathbf{a}_2, \mathbf{a}_3$. Determine if $\mathbf{b}$ is a linear combination of these columns.

Solution

Row reduce the augmented matrix:

$$\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 4 & 2 & | & 3 \\ 0 & 3 & 5 & | & 7 \\ 2 & 8 & 4 & | & 3 \end{bmatrix} \sim \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 4 & 2 & | & 3 \\ 0 & 3 & 5 & | & 7 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & | & -3 \end{bmatrix}$$

The last row gives $0 = -3$, which is a contradiction. Therefore the system is inconsistent.

$\mathbf{b}$ is NOT a linear combination of the columns of $A$.
