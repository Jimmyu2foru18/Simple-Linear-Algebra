Section 1.5: Solution Sets of Linear Systems

Assigned problems: 1, 3, my problems. Source: linearAlgebraSolutionsComplete.pdf.

What is this section about?

What is a solution set? The solution set of a linear system is the set of all vectors x that satisfy Ax = b. When free variables exist, the solution set can be written in parametric vector form.

Core Lecture, Gilbert Strang (MIT)

Concept Overview

A homogeneous system $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{0}$ always has at least the trivial solution $\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{0}$. It has nontrivial solutions if and only if there is at least one free variable.

The general solution of a consistent system $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$ can be written in parametric vector form:

$$\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{p} + x_{f_1}\mathbf{v}_1 + x_{f_2}\mathbf{v}_2 + \cdots$$

where $\mathbf{p}$ is a particular solution and $\mathbf{v}_i$ are vectors corresponding to free variables.

Worked Problems from linearAlgebraSolutionsComplete.pdf

Problem 1

Problem: Determine whether the homogeneous system $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{0}$ has a nontrivial solution. Row reduce the augmented matrix to find pivot positions.

Solution

Row reducing:

$$\begin{bmatrix} 2 & 5 & 8 & | & 0 \\ 2 & 7 & 1 & | & 0 \\ 4 & 2 & 7 & | & 0 \end{bmatrix} \sim \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 5 & 8 & | & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 & | & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & | & 0 \end{bmatrix}$$

The variable $x_3$ is free (column 3 is not a pivot column). Therefore the system has nontrivial solutions.

Problem 3

Problem: Determine whether the homogeneous system $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{0}$ has a nontrivial solution.

Solution

Row reducing:

$$\begin{bmatrix} 3 & 5 & 7 & | & 0 \\ 6 & 7 & 1 & | & 0 \end{bmatrix} \sim \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 5 & 7 & | & 0 \\ 0 & -3 & -11 & | & 0 \end{bmatrix}$$

The variable $x_3$ is free. With only 2 equations and 3 variables, at most 2 variables can be basic, so at least 1 must be free. The system has nontrivial solutions.
