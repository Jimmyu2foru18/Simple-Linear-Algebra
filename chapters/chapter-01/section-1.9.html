Section 1.9: The Matrix of a Linear Transformation

Assigned problems: 1, 7, 8. Source: linearAlgebraSolutionsComplete.pdf.

What is this section about?

What is the matrix of a linear transformation? Every linear transformation T: R⁻¹ → R⁻ can be represented as matrix multiplication T(x) = Ax, where A is the standard matrix whose columns are T(e₁), …, T(e₀).

Core Lecture, Gilbert Strang (MIT)

Concept Overview

If $T: \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}^m$ is a linear transformation, then $T(\mathbf{x}) = A\mathbf{x}$ for a unique matrix $A$, called the standard matrix of $T$.

The columns of $A$ are the images of the standard basis vectors:

$$A = [T(\mathbf{e}_1) \, T(\mathbf{e}_2) \, \dots \, T(\mathbf{e}_n)]$$

Worked Problems from linearAlgebraSolutionsComplete.pdf

Problem 1

Problem: Find the standard matrix $A = [T(\mathbf{e}_1) \, T(\mathbf{e}_2)]$ of the linear transformation $T$.

Solution

$$A = \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 5 \\ 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$$

(A $4 \times 2$ matrix whose columns are $T(\mathbf{e}_1)$ and $T(\mathbf{e}_2)$.)

Problem 7

Problem: Find the standard matrix of the transformation that rotates by $-3\pi/4$ radians and then reflects across the horizontal axis.

Solution

Following what happens to $\mathbf{e}_1$ and $\mathbf{e}_2$:

  • $\mathbf{e}_1 = (1, 0)$ rotates to $(-1/\sqrt{2}, -1/\sqrt{2})$, then reflects to $(-1/\sqrt{2}, 1/\sqrt{2})$
  • $\mathbf{e}_2 = (0, 1)$ rotates to $(-1/\sqrt{2}, -1/\sqrt{2})$, then reflects to $(-1/\sqrt{2}, 1/\sqrt{2})$

Standard matrix: $$A = \begin{bmatrix} -1/2 & 1/2 \\ 1/2 & 1/2 \end{bmatrix}$$

Problem: Find the standard matrix $A$ of the transformation.

Solution

$$A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 2 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$$
