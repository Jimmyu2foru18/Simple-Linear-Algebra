Section 1.7: Linear Independence

Assigned problems: 1, 7, 11, 13. Source: linearAlgebraSolutionsComplete.pdf.

What is this section about?

What is linear independence? A set of vectors is linearly independent if the equation c₁v₁ + c₂v₂ + … + c₀v₀ = 0 has only the trivial solution c₁ = c₂ = … = c₀ = 0.

Core Lecture, Gilbert Strang (MIT)

Concept Overview

A set of vectors $\{\mathbf{v}_1, \dots, \mathbf{v}_p\}$ in $\mathbb{R}^n$ is linearly independent if the vector equation:

$$x_1\mathbf{v}_1 + x_2\mathbf{v}_2 + \cdots + x_p\mathbf{v}_p = \mathbf{0}$$

has only the trivial solution ($x_1 = x_2 = \cdots = x_p = 0$).

Equivalently, the columns of the matrix $A = [\mathbf{v}_1 \, \dots \, \mathbf{v}_p]$ are linearly independent if and only if $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{0}$ has only the trivial solution, which happens when every column is a pivot column.

Problem 1

Problem: Determine whether the given vectors are linearly independent by studying the solution set of $x_1\mathbf{u} + x_2\mathbf{v} + x_3\mathbf{w} = \mathbf{0}$ using an augmented matrix.

Solution

Row reducing the augmented matrix:

$$\begin{bmatrix} 5 & 7 & 9 & | & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & 4 & | & 0 \\ 0 & 6 & 8 & | & 0 \end{bmatrix} \sim \begin{bmatrix} 5 & 7 & 9 & | & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & 4 & | & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & | & 0 \end{bmatrix}$$

Wait — actually, the reduction shows there are no free variables (every column is a pivot column).

The homogeneous equation has only the trivial solution. The vectors are linearly independent.

Problem 7

Problem: Determine whether the columns of $A$ are linearly independent by studying $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{0}$.

Solution

$A$ is a $3 \times 4$ matrix (3 rows, 4 columns). There are at most 3 pivot positions (one per row). With 4 variables and at most 3 pivots, at least one variable must be free.

The equation $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{0}$ has a nontrivial solution. The columns of $A$ are linearly dependent.

Problem 11

Problem: For what value of $h$ are the vectors $\mathbf{v}_1, \mathbf{v}_2, \mathbf{v}_3$ linearly dependent?

Solution

Row reducing $[\mathbf{v}_1 \, \mathbf{v}_2 \, \mathbf{v}_3 \, | \, \mathbf{0}]$:

$$\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 3 & 1 & | & 0 \\ 1 & 5 & 5 & | & 0 \\ 4 & 7 & 0 & | & 0 \end{bmatrix} \sim \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 3 & 1 & | & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & 4 & | & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & h-6 & | & 0 \end{bmatrix}$$

If $h - 6 = 0$ (i.e., $h = 6$), then $x_3$ is a free variable and there is a nontrivial solution.

The vectors are linearly dependent if and only if $h = 6$.

Problem 13

Problem: For what value of $h$ are the vectors $\mathbf{v}_1, \mathbf{v}_2, \mathbf{v}_3$ linearly dependent?

Solution

Row reducing $[\mathbf{v}_1 \, \mathbf{v}_2 \, \mathbf{v}_3 \, | \, \mathbf{0}]$:

$$\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 & | & 0 \\ 5 & 9 & 0 & | & 0 \\ 3 & 6 & 0 & | & 0 \end{bmatrix} \sim \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 & | & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 & | & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & | & 0 \end{bmatrix}$$

The third row is all zeros regardless of $h$, so $x_3$ is always a free variable. There is always a nontrivial solution.

The vectors are linearly dependent for all values of $h$.
