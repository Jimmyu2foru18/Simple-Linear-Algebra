What is this section about?

What is a null space? The null space of a matrix A is the set of all solutions to Ax = 0, while the column space is the set of all linear combinations of the columns of A.

Core Lecture, Gilbert Strang (MIT)

Concept Overview

The null space of an $m \times n$ matrix $A$, written $\text{Nul } A$, is the set of all solutions to the homogeneous equation $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{0}$.

The column space of $A$, written $\text{Col } A$, is the set of all linear combinations of the columns of $A$.

To find a spanning set for $\text{Nul } A$, row reduce $[A \, | \, \mathbf{0}]$ and express the general solution in parametric vector form.

Worked Problems from linearAlgebraSolutionsComplete.pdf

Problem 1

Problem: Show that $\mathbf{w}$ is in $\text{Nul } A$ where $A = \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 5 & 3 \\ 6 & 2 & 0 \\ 8 & 4 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$.

Solution

$$A\mathbf{w} = \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 5 & 3 \\ 6 & 2 & 0 \\ 8 & 4 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ -1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix} = \mathbf{0}$$

So $\mathbf{w}$ is in $\text{Nul } A$.

Problem 3

Problem: Find a spanning set for $\text{Nul } A$ where $A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 7 & 6 \\ 0 & 1 & 4 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$.

Solution

The general solution is $x_1 = -7x_3 - 6x_4$, $x_2 = -4x_3 - 2x_4$, with $x_3$ and $x_4$ free.

$$\mathbf{x} = x_3\begin{bmatrix} -7 \\ -4 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix} + x_4\begin{bmatrix} -6 \\ -2 \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$$

A spanning set for $\text{Nul } A$ is $\left\{\begin{bmatrix} -7 \\ -4 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}, \begin{bmatrix} -6 \\ -2 \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}\right\}$.

Problem 5

Problem: Find a spanning set for $\text{Nul } A$ where $A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 0 & 4 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 9 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$.

Solution

The general solution is $x_1 = -2x_2 - 4x_4$, $x_3 = -9x_4$, $x_5 = 0$, with $x_2$ and $x_4$ free.

$$\mathbf{x} = x_2\begin{bmatrix} -2 \\ 1 \\ 0 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix} + x_4\begin{bmatrix} -4 \\ 0 \\ -9 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}$$

A spanning set for $\text{Nul } A$ is $\left\{\begin{bmatrix} -2 \\ 1 \\ 0 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}, \begin{bmatrix} -4 \\ 0 \\ -9 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}\right\}$.

Problem 11

Problem: Find a basis for $\text{Nul } A$ where $A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$.

Solution

The general solution is $x = -2y - z$ with $y$ and $z$ free.

$$\mathbf{x} = y\begin{bmatrix} -2 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix} + z\begin{bmatrix} -1 \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$$

A basis for $\text{Nul } A$ is $\left\{\begin{bmatrix} -2 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}, \begin{bmatrix} -1 \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}\right\}$.

Problem 12

Problem: Find a basis for $\text{Nul } A$ where $A = \begin{bmatrix} 5 & -1 \end{bmatrix}$.

Solution

The general solution is $y = 5x$ with $x$ free.

$$\mathbf{x} = x\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 5 \end{bmatrix}$$

A basis for $\text{Nul } A$ is $\left\{\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 5 \end{bmatrix}\right\}$.

Problem 13

Problem: Find a basis for $\text{Col } A$ where the first and second columns of $A$ are pivot columns.

Solution

Since $B$ is a row echelon form of $A$, the first and second columns of $A$ are its pivot columns. Thus a basis for $\text{Col } A$ is:

$$\left\{\begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 3 \end{bmatrix}, \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 6 \end{bmatrix}\right\}$$

A basis for $\text{Col } A$ is $\left\{\begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 3 \end{bmatrix}, \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 6 \end{bmatrix}\right\}$.

Problem 15

Problem: Since the zero vector is not in $W$, determine if $W$ is a vector space.

Solution

Since the zero vector is not in $W$, $W$ is not a vector space.
