What is this section about?

What is a coordinate system? Given a basis B for a vector space, the coordinate vector of x relative to B lists the coefficients when x is written as a linear combination of the basis vectors.

Core Lecture, Gilbert Strang (MIT)

Concept Overview

Let $\mathcal{B} = \{\mathbf{b}_1, \dots, \mathbf{b}_n\}$ be a basis for a vector space $V$. For any $\mathbf{x}$ in $V$, the coordinate vector of $\mathbf{x}$ relative to $\mathcal{B}$ is:

$$[\mathbf{x}]_{\mathcal{B}} = \begin{bmatrix} c_1 \\ c_2 \\ \vdots \\ c_n \end{bmatrix}$$

where $\mathbf{x} = c_1\mathbf{b}_1 + c_2\mathbf{b}_2 + \cdots + c_n\mathbf{b}_n$.

The change-of-coordinates matrix from $\mathcal{B}$ to the standard basis is:

$$P_{\mathcal{B}} = [\mathbf{b}_1 \, \mathbf{b}_2 \, \dots \, \mathbf{b}_n]$$
