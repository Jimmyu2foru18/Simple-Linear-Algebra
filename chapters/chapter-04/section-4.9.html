What is this section about?

What is a Markov chain? A Markov chain models transitions between states using a stochastic matrix whose columns are probability vectors summing to 1.

Core Lecture, Gilbert Strang (MIT)

Concept Overview

A stochastic matrix is a square matrix whose columns are probability vectors (nonnegative entries that sum to 1).

A Markov chain is described by:

$$\mathbf{x}_{k+1} = P\mathbf{x}_k$$

where $P$ is a stochastic matrix and $\mathbf{x}_k$ is the state vector at step $k$.

A steady-state vector $\mathbf{q}$ satisfies $P\mathbf{q} = \mathbf{q}$, equivalently $(P - I)\mathbf{q} = \mathbf{0}$.
