Section 4.7: Change of Basis

What is this section about?

What is change of basis? Change of basis converts coordinates of a vector relative to one basis into coordinates relative to another basis using a change-of-coordinates matrix.

Core Lecture, Gilbert Strang (MIT)

Concept Overview

If $\mathcal{B} = \{\mathbf{b}_1, \dots, \mathbf{b}_n\}$ and $\mathcal{C} = \{\mathbf{c}_1, \dots, \mathbf{c}_n\}$ are bases for a vector space $V$, then the change-of-coordinates matrix from $\mathcal{B}$ to $\mathcal{C}$ is:

$$P_{\mathcal{C}\leftarrow\mathcal{B}} = [[\mathbf{b}_1]_{\mathcal{C}} \, \dots \, [\mathbf{b}_n]_{\mathcal{C}}]$$

This matrix satisfies: $[\mathbf{x}]_{\mathcal{C}} = P_{\mathcal{C}\leftarrow\mathcal{B}}[\mathbf{x}]_{\mathcal{B}}$

The change-of-coordinates matrix is invertible, with $(P_{\mathcal{C}\leftarrow\mathcal{B}})^{-1} = P_{\mathcal{B}\leftarrow\mathcal{C}}$.

Worked Problems from linearAlgebraSolutionsComplete.pdf

Problem 1

Problem: Find the change-of-coordinates matrix from $\mathcal{B}$ to the standard basis in $\mathbb{R}^2$.

Solution

The change-of-coordinates matrix from $\mathcal{B}$ to the standard basis in $\mathbb{R}^2$ is:

$$P = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$$

Problem 3

Problem: Find the change-of-coordinates matrix from $\mathcal{B}$ to the standard basis in $\mathbb{R}^3$.

Solution

The change-of-coordinates matrix from $\mathcal{B}$ to the standard basis in $\mathbb{R}^3$ is:

$$P = \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 2 & 8 \\ 1 & 0 & 2 \\ 4 & 5 & 7 \end{bmatrix}$$
