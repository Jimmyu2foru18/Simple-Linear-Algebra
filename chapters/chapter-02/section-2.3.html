Section 2.3: Cramer's Rule and Volume

Assigned problems: 15, 24, 26. Source: linearAlgebraSolutionsComplete.pdf (PDF Chapter 3, Section 3.3).

What is this section about?

What is Cramer’s Rule? Cramer’s Rule gives a formula for solving Ax = b using determinants, and |det A| equals the area or volume of the parallelepiped determined by the columns of A.

Core Lecture, Gilbert Strang (MIT)

Concept Overview

Cramer's Rule: If $A$ is an invertible $n \times n$ matrix, the solution to $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$ has entries:

$$x_i = \frac{\det A_i(\mathbf{b})}{\det A}$$

where $A_i(\mathbf{b})$ is the matrix formed by replacing column $i$ of $A$ with the vector $\mathbf{b}$.

Geometric interpretation: The absolute value of the determinant gives the area (2D) or volume (3D) of the parallelogram/parallelepiped determined by the columns of $A$.

Problem 15

Problem: Given $\det A = 6$ and the cofactors of $A$, compute $\text{adj } A$ and $A^{-1}$.

Solution

The adjugate matrix is:

$$\text{adj } A = \begin{bmatrix} 20 & 0 \\ 2 & 6 \\ 19 & 3 \end{bmatrix}$$

And the inverse is:

$$A^{-1} = \frac{1}{\det A} \text{adj } A = \frac{1}{6} \begin{bmatrix} 20 & 0 \\ 2 & 6 \\ 19 & 3 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 11/3 & 0 \\ 1/3 & 1 \\ 0 & 1/2 \end{bmatrix}$$

Problem 24

Problem: Find the volume of the parallelepiped determined by the columns of $A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 4 & 5 & 2 \\ 0 & 2 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$.

Solution

The volume of the parallelepiped is $|\det A|$.

$$\det A = -15$$

Volume $= |-15| = 15$.

Problem 26

Problem: Show that a linear transformation $T$ maps the set $p + S$ into the set $T(p) + T(S)$.

Solution

By definition, $p + S$ is the set of all vectors of the form $p + v$ where $v$ is in $S$. Applying $T$:

$$T(p + v) = T(p) + T(v)$$

This vector is in $T(p) + T(S)$. Conversely, any vector in $T(p) + T(S)$ has the form $T(p) + T(v) = T(p + v)$ for some $v$ in $S$.

Therefore $T$ maps $p + S$ onto $T(p) + T(S)$.
