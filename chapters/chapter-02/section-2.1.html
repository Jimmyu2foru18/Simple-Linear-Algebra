Section 2.1: Introduction to Determinants

Assigned problems: 5, 15, 16, 21, 23, 33. Source: linearAlgebraSolutionsComplete.pdf (PDF Chapter 3, Section 3.1).

What is this section about?

What is a determinant? The determinant of a square matrix is a scalar value computed from its entries that reveals key properties of the matrix, such as whether it is invertible.

Concept Overview

The determinant of an $n \times n$ matrix $A$, denoted $\det A$ or $|A|$, is a scalar value computed from the entries of $A$. For a $2 \times 2$ matrix:

$$\det \begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix} = ad - bc$$

For larger matrices, the determinant is computed by cofactor expansion along any row or column:

$$\det A = \sum_{j=1}^{n} (-1)^{i+j} a_{ij} \det A_{ij}$$

where $A_{ij}$ is the $(n-1) \times (n-1)$ submatrix obtained by deleting row $i$ and column $j$.

A key fact: if $A$ is triangular (upper or lower), then $\det A$ is the product of its diagonal entries.

Problem 5

Problem: Compute the determinant by cofactor expansion along the first row (and verify along the second column).

Solution

Compute $\det A$ by expanding along the first row, then verify by expanding along the second column. Both expansions should give the same value.

Problem 15

Problem: Compute the determinant of the matrix $\begin{bmatrix} 3 & 0 & 4 \\ 2 & 3 & 2 \\ 0 & 5 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$.

Solution

$$\det A = -(3)(3)(-1) + (0)(2)(0) + (4)(2)(5) - (0)(3)(4) - (5)(2)(3) - (-1)(2)(0)$$

$$= -9 + 0 + 40 - 0 - 30 - 0 = 1$$

$\det A = 1$.

Problem 16

Problem: Compute the determinant of the matrix $\begin{bmatrix} 0 & 5 & 1 \\ 4 & 3 & 0 \\ 2 & 4 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$.

Solution

$$\det A = (0)(-3)(1) + (5)(0)(2) + (1)(4)(4) - (2)(-3)(1) - (4)(0)(0) - (1)(4)(5)$$

$$= 0 + 0 + 16 - (-6) - 0 - 20 = 2$$

$\det A = 2$.

Problem 21

Problem: Given a matrix and a row operation replacing row 2 with $k$ times row 1 plus row 2, show the determinant is unchanged.

Solution

$$\begin{bmatrix} 3 & 4 & 1 & | & 8 \\ 2 & 5 & 6 & | & 2 \\ 5 & 6 & 3 & | & 4 \end{bmatrix}$$

The row operation replaces row 2 with $k$ times row 1 plus row 2, and the determinant is unchanged.

Problem 23

Problem: Given the matrix $\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 3 & 8 & 4 \\ 2 & 3 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$ with row 1 scaled by $k$, compute the determinant.

Solution

$$\det A = 1(4) - 1(2) + 1(-7) = 4 - 2 - 7 = -5$$

After scaling row 1 by $k$, the determinant is multiplied by $k$.

The row operation scales row 1 by $k$, and the determinant is multiplied by $k$.

Problem 33

Problem: Given elementary matrix $E = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$ and $A = \begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix}$, show that $\det(EA) = (\det E)(\det A)$.

Solution

$$\det E = -1, \quad \det A = ad - bc$$

$$EA = \begin{bmatrix} c & d \\ a & b \end{bmatrix}, \quad \det(EA) = cb - da = -(ad - bc)$$

$$\det(EA) = -1(ad - bc) = (\det E)(\det A)$$

The determinant of a product equals the product of the determinants.
