Section 2.2: Properties of Determinants

Assigned problems: 1, 3, 5. Source: linearAlgebraSolutionsComplete.pdf (PDF Chapter 3, Section 3.2).

What is this section about?

What are properties of determinants? Elementary row operations affect the determinant predictably: row replacement leaves it unchanged, row interchange changes its sign, and row scaling multiplies it by the scalar.

Core Lecture, Gilbert Strang (MIT)

Concept Overview

Elementary row operations affect the determinant in predictable ways (Theorems 3a–3c):

  • Row replacement: Adding a multiple of one row to another does not change the determinant.
  • Row interchange: Swapping two rows multiplies the determinant by $-1$ (changes sign).
  • Row scaling: Multiplying a row by a scalar $k$ multiplies the determinant by $k$.

If $A$ is reduced to echelon form $U$ using $r$ row interchanges and scaling factors $k_1, \dots, k_p$, then:

$$\det A = (-1)^r \frac{\text{(product of pivots of } U)}{k_1 \cdots k_p}$$

Worked Problems from linearAlgebraSolutionsComplete.pdf

Problem 1

Problem: Rows 1 and 2 are interchanged. What happens to the determinant?

Solution

By Theorem 3b, interchanging two rows multiplies the determinant by $-1$.

The determinant changes sign.

Problem 3

Problem: A row replacement operation is performed. What happens to the determinant?

Solution

By Theorem 3a, a row replacement operation (adding a multiple of one row to another) does not change the determinant.

The determinant is unchanged.

Problem: Compute the determinant of the matrix $\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 5 & 6 \\ 1 & 4 & 0 \\ 2 & 7 & 9 \end{bmatrix}$ using row operations.
