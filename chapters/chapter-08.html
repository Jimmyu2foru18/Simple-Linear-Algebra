Detailed Topic Breakdowns

Complete Solution Structure

The complete solution to $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$ has the form $\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{x}_p + \mathbf{x}_n$. One particular solution $\mathbf{x}_p$ satisfies $A\mathbf{x}_p = \mathbf{b}$. Adding any nullspace vector $\mathbf{x}_n$ (where $A\mathbf{x}_n = \mathbf{0}$) gives another solution. The full solution set is an affine subspace.

Existence Conditions

$A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$ has a solution if and only if $\mathbf{b}$ is in $C(A)$. Equivalently, $\text{rank}(A) = \text{rank}([A \mid \mathbf{b}])$. If the augmented matrix has an extra nonzero row compared to $A$ (e.g., $[0 \; \cdots \; 0 \mid c]$ where $c \neq 0$), the system is inconsistent.

Row Reduced Form R

$R$ is the RREF of $A$. It reveals pivot and free variables and makes the structure of solutions transparent. The RREF of $[A \mid \mathbf{b}]$ directly shows whether a solution exists and provides a particular solution.

Particular Solutions

To find one particular solution, set all free variables to zero in the RREF of $[A \mid \mathbf{b}]$. The pivot variables are then determined by the rightmost column of the reduced augmented matrix.

Computation Examples

Derivation: The General Solution

Let $\mathbf{x}_p$ be any particular solution to $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$. For any $\mathbf{x}_n \in N(A)$:

$$A(\mathbf{x}_p + \mathbf{x}_n) = A\mathbf{x}_p + A\mathbf{x}_n = \mathbf{b} + \mathbf{0} = \mathbf{b}$$

Therefore, $\mathbf{x}_p + \mathbf{x}_n$ is also a solution.

Conversely, if $\mathbf{x}$ is any solution:

$$A(\mathbf{x} - \mathbf{x}_p) = A\mathbf{x} - A\mathbf{x}_p = \mathbf{b} - \mathbf{b} = \mathbf{0}$$

So $\mathbf{x} - \mathbf{x}_p \in N(A)$, meaning $\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{x}_p + \mathbf{x}_n$ for some $\mathbf{x}_n$.

This proves the complete solution is exactly the particular solution plus the entire nullspace.

Derivation: Inconsistency Detection

After row reduction, if any row of $[R \mid \mathbf{d}]$ has the form $[0 \; 0 \; \cdots \; 0 \mid c]$ where $c \neq 0$, then:

$$0 \cdot x_1 + 0 \cdot x_2 + \cdots + 0 \cdot x_n = c$$ $$0 = c \quad \text{(impossible!)}$$

This means $\mathbf{b} \notin C(A)$, so $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$ has no solution.

Learning Strategies

Mnemonic Devices

Mnemonic: "One Particular Plus All Nullspace"

The complete solution is not just one answer — it's one particular solution plus the entire nullspace. Think of it as: "Find one solution, then add all the ways to get zero."

Mnemonic: "0 = 0 is OK, 0 = c is Not"

In the RREF of $[A \mid \mathbf{b}]$, a row of all zeros in $A$ is fine. But if that row has a nonzero entry in the $\mathbf{b}$-column, the system is inconsistent (no solution).

Study Tips

  • Always check consistency first by comparing $\text{rank}(A)$ and $\text{rank}([A \mid \mathbf{b}])$
  • When finding a particular solution, set free variables to zero
  • Write the complete solution as: $\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{x}_p + c_1\mathbf{v}_1 + c_2\mathbf{v}_2 + \cdots$
  • Verify by substituting the particular solution back into $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$
  • The number of special solutions equals the number of free variables

Visual Aids

The solution set of $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$ is the particular solution shifted by the nullspace:

$$N(A) = \text{line through origin in } \mathbb{R}^3$$ $$\mathbf{x}_p = \text{specific point not on that line}$$ $$\text{Solution set} = \text{line through } \mathbf{x}_p \text{ parallel to } N(A)$$

All solutions: $\mathbf{x}_p + N(A) = \{\mathbf{x}_p + \mathbf{x}_n : \mathbf{x}_n \in N(A)\}$

$$\text{RREF}(A): \quad \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$$ $$\text{RREF}([A \mid \mathbf{b}]): \quad \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 2 & \mid & 3 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 & \mid & 2 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & \mid & 1 \end{bmatrix} \quad \Rightarrow \quad 0 = 1 \text{? INCONSISTENT!}$$

Worked Examples

Example 1: Consistent System

Problem: Solve $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$ completely for:

$$A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 2 \\ 2 & 4 & 6 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \mathbf{b} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 5 \end{bmatrix}$$

Solution:

  1. RREF of $[A \mid \mathbf{b}]$: $\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 2 & \mid & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 2 & \mid & 3 \end{bmatrix}$
  2. Consistent (no $0 = \text{nonzero}$ row)
  3. Pivot variables: $x_1, x_3$. Free: $x_2$
  4. Particular solution ($x_2 = 0$): $x_1 = -2, x_3 = 1.5$
  5. Special solution ($x_2 = 1$): $x_1 = -2, x_3 = 0$
  6. Complete: $\mathbf{x} = \begin{bmatrix} -2 \\ 0 \\ 1.5 \end{bmatrix} + c \begin{bmatrix} -2 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}$

Example 2: Inconsistent System

Problem: Determine if $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$ has a solution for:

$$A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 4 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \mathbf{b} = \begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ 7 \end{bmatrix}$$

Solution:

  • RREF of $[A \mid \mathbf{b}]$: $\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & \mid & 3 \\ 0 & 0 & \mid & 1 \end{bmatrix}$
  • Second row: $0 = 1$ is impossible
  • $\mathbf{b} \notin C(A)$. No solution exists.

Practice Problems

Problem Set 8

Problem 1: Solve $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$ completely for:

$$A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 & 3 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \mathbf{b} = \begin{bmatrix} 6 \\ 14 \end{bmatrix}$$

Hint: Check consistency, then find particular and nullspace parts.

Solution

RREF: $\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & -1 & \mid & -2 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 & \mid & 8 \end{bmatrix}$

Particular: $\mathbf{x} = \begin{bmatrix} -2 \\ 8 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}$ (setting free $x_3 = 0$)

Special: $\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ -2 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$

Complete: $\mathbf{x} = \begin{bmatrix} -2 \\ 8 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix} + c \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ -2 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$

Problem 2: For which values of $b_1, b_2$ does $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$ have a solution?

$$A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 3 \\ 2 & 6 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \mathbf{b} = \begin{bmatrix} b_1 \\ b_2 \end{bmatrix}$$

Hint: The column space is a line. What condition must $\mathbf{b}$ satisfy?

Solution

$C(A) = \text{span}\{\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix}\}$. So $b_2 = 2b_1$ is required. If $b_2 \neq 2b_1$, no solution.

Problem 3: If $\mathbf{x}_{p1}$ and $\mathbf{x}_{p2}$ are two particular solutions to $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$, show that their difference is in $N(A)$.

Hint: Subtract the two equations $A\mathbf{x}_{p1} = \mathbf{b}$ and $A\mathbf{x}_{p2} = \mathbf{b}$.

Solution $$A(\mathbf{x}_{p1} - \mathbf{x}_{p2}) = A\mathbf{x}_{p1} - A\mathbf{x}_{p2} = \mathbf{b} - \mathbf{b} = \mathbf{0}$$

So $\mathbf{x}_{p1} - \mathbf{x}_{p2} \in N(A)$.

Problem 4: Write the complete solution to $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{0}$ and $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$ when $A$ is invertible.

Hint: If $A$ is invertible, what does RREF look like?

Solution

If $A$ is invertible, $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{0}$ has only $\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{0}$. For $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$, the unique solution is $\mathbf{x} = A^{-1}\mathbf{b}$. There are no free variables, so nullspace is $\{\mathbf{0}\}$ and the particular solution is unique.
